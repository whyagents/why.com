Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 20, 2026

Ockams, Inc. ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, why.com (the "Site"), and submit your information for early access.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the site.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information that identifies, relates to, describes, or could reasonably be linked, directly or indirectly, with a particular consumer or device. Specifically, we collect:

Personal Identifiers: Name and Phone Number (collected only when you voluntarily submit the Early Access form).

Professional Information: Your selected profile/affiliation (e.g., Founder, Family Office, Operator) and your responses to open-ended questions.

Internet or Other Electronic Network Activity: IP address, browser type, operating system, and data regarding your interactions with our Site (e.g., button clicks, scrolling).

Curiosity Paths: Saved paths are stored locally in your browser. When you ask a question or choose a follow-up, WHY sends the current question and up to 12 recent messages from the active path to our answer service and model provider so the answer can retain context. After meaningful use, WHY may also send one compact, non-sensitive path cue—topic words plus a short mechanism and named entity—to shape the third follow-up question. It does not send the earlier questions, answers, or transcripts from other paths. Resetting your curiosity profile disables these connections until they are earned again.

Daily WHY Choices: To show how recorded explorers divided across the same daily paths, WHY creates a random token for each daily episode on your device. When you choose a daily path, we store a one-way hash of that token with the episode, question node, chosen path and timestamp. We do not attach your name, typed questions, answer history or curiosity transcript to this aggregate choice record. The token rotates with each daily episode and is used to limit the browser to one counted choice per question node.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

Evaluate your request for early access to the WHY platform.

Communicate with you via SMS/text message or phone call regarding your invitation and platform updates.

Monitor and analyze usage and trends to improve your experience with our Site.

Deliver targeted advertising to you across third-party platforms (like Meta) based on your interactions with our Site.

3. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies, web beacons, and similar tracking technologies (including the Meta Pixel and Google Analytics 4) to track activity on our Site.

Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand how users engage with our Site. We have implemented Data Redaction and disabled form-scraping to ensure your raw personal contact information is never sent to our analytics providers.

Advertising: We use the Meta Pixel to measure the effectiveness of our ad campaigns.

Consent Management: We utilize a Consent Management Platform (CMP) to respect your tracking preferences. Tracking technologies will only deploy if you grant consent via our cookie banner.

4. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information for monetary value. However, under California law (CCPA/CPRA), sharing data with advertising platforms (like Meta) for targeted advertising may be considered a "sale" or "sharing." We share data with:

Service Providers: Companies that assist us with analytics (Google) and communications infrastructure.

Advertising Partners: Platforms like Meta to help us deliver relevant ads.

5. Your Privacy Rights (CCPA & GDPR)

Depending on your location (such as California or the European Economic Area), you have the right to:

Access: Request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

Deletion: Request that we delete your personal data.

Correction: Request that we correct inaccurate personal data.

Opt-Out of "Sale" or "Sharing": You can opt out of targeted advertising cookies via our cookie banner. Furthermore, our website automatically detects and honors the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal broadcast by your browser.

6. Contact Us

To exercise any of your privacy rights, or if you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: Ockams, Inc. Email: privacy@ockams.com
