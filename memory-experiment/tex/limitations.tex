\paragraph{We built the environment.} We designed the stressors, and the method was designed with them in mind. The Clean scenario, the ablations, and the sweeps show which assumptions carry the effect: channel metadata that says which outcomes were verified, outcomes that never arrive, shocks that hit many decisions at once, and scarce memory. The share of late outcomes mattered less than we expected. An environment without those properties would show a smaller advantage, as Clean does.

\paragraph{The agent is small.} A 13-parameter logistic model learns from a few hundred examples and does not suffer the interference, representational drift, or capacity effects of neural networks. Nothing here tests consolidation into LLM weights or retrieval-based memory for LLM agents.

\paragraph{Forgetting is not free.} Unbounded memory beat the method in every stationary scenario, including both poisoning scenarios, where 200 poisoned episodes were diluted by thousands of honest ones. Consequence-weighting was also worse calibrated than random retention, mostly because of the surprise factor. Importance weighting or a small random calibration reservoir are natural fixes that we did not test.

\paragraph{Verification is a trust boundary.} The method's robustness to poisoning came mostly from discounting manipulable channels. When false outcomes arrived as verified, it consolidated them at about \val{pratio-poison_verified-why} times the rate of honest episodes. ``Reality is the reward model'' is a useful slogan and an incomplete one: outcomes are measurements, measurements can be gamed, and optimizing any outcome metric invites Goodhart effects \citep{manheim2018goodhart}.

\paragraph{Causal confidence is crude.} Our confound detector only notices day-level shocks. Confounders that affect a subset of decisions, or that correlate with the action, pass straight through. It also slowed adaptation in the first days after a real change, which initially looks like a shock. Real deployments need attribution by design: randomized exploration, holdouts, and off-policy estimation over the alternatives that were not chosen.

\paragraph{The score is over-specified.} Three of its five factors (surprise, consequence, transferability) did not earn their place in this environment. A different environment could change that; we did not revise the score after seeing the ablations, and Experiment~2 tests a reduced score as a secondary arm.

\paragraph{Simulated raters and users.} Self-rated importance is a noisy function of drama and stakes, not a language model, and approval is a logistic function of agreeableness and quality, not a person. The oracle is a greedy approximation. The WHY constants were fixed a priori and the baselines were not tuned either; tuned baselines might narrow the gap. Experiment~2 is designed to remove these simplifications.
