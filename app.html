One Private Curiosity Graph

Continuity, without surrender.

Questions, decisions, notes, and outcomes should not become isolated transcripts. WHY connects them locally so the useful parts can influence what comes next. This private graph serves its owner; it does not become population training data by default. Optional synchronization is being designed to move permissioned context—not an unrestricted copy of a person’s life.
